Appearance

Anouran is a lean man of 49 with dark hair, kept short and neat, and a face lined more by thought than by laughter. He dresses well, in dark, perfectly cut cloth he stitches himself. The tailoring is so good that nobody would guess he grew up with patched elbows. He has no holy symbol, no church colors and no trinkets. His hands are steady, his voice is quiet, and he has the habit of looking at people as if he's measuring them for something. Usually it's only a coat. A scruffy, loyal mutt named Maynard is never far from his heels.

Biography

Anouran was the only child of a poor family in Duskwood. His father cut timber and his mother took in mending, and what little they had went to keeping the lanterns lit and the door barred. Other children in the village feared the dark. Anouran never did. Even as a boy, the shadows between the trees felt less like a threat and more like a presence that was paying attention to him.

One summer, when he was eight, his father found seasonal work in Elwynn, and the family went with him. Anouran went swimming in Crystal Lake. The water was bright and clear, and something beneath it took hold of his ankle and dragged him down. He remembers the sunlight shrinking above him, his lungs burning, and then a cold, dark pressure wrapping around him like a hand and pulling him up. He came out on the shore alone, coughing, with no one near enough to have saved him. He never told his parents. He knew what had answered him, and he knew that it had come from inside himself.

He has never gone back to the lake. He isn't afraid of the darkness that saved him. He's afraid of what was waiting under the water, and of how close he came to dying in all that light.

As a young man he left Duskwood for Stormwind with nothing but a needle and his mother's skills. He built a living as a tailor, slowly and carefully, until he could afford an apartment high above the street. In the quiet hours he taught himself the shadow, through study, meditation and patience. He has never set foot in the Cathedral's classrooms. The Light has its servants. The shadow chose him, and he answers only to it.

Through tailoring he met Sister Pfister, and she has become one of his dearest friends. They trade patterns, talk late into the evening and look out for each other. Neither one knows what the other keeps buried. Each suspects there is something there, and each is too polite, or too careful, to ask.

Personality

Anouran is patient, composed and deliberate. He considers himself a good man, and in most ways he is: generous to his few friends, kind to strays, and honest in his trade. But he has watched the Alliance pay for its hesitation for years. The Horde grows bolder at its borders, the king is gone, and Stormwind's court debates while soldiers die. He believes the Alliance needs a hand that isn't afraid to close into a fist. In time, he intends for that hand to be his.

He has lines he will not cross. He doesn't harm the innocent, he doesn't betray his friends, and he won't take power by means he couldn't defend to his own conscience. Shadow is a tool, not a master, or so he tells himself.

In character

  • Rumor in the Trade District says the Cathedral threw him out for heresy. He never set foot in the place. He has never bothered to correct the story.
  • He found Maynard as a starving stray puppy in an alley in Stormwind. The dog has eaten better than he has ever since.
  • He gives Goldshire a wide berth, because Crystal Lake is too close.
  • He can often be found in a quiet corner with one glass of red wine, listening to soldiers and dock workers talk about the war more than he talks himself.
  • When Sister Pfister visits, the door is always open and the good wine comes out.
