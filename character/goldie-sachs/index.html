Appearance

Goldie is a tall, broad-shouldered man of 42, and the first thing anyone notices is his hair. It is long, thick and golden, falls past his shoulders, and is never once out of place. He keeps a neat moustache and goatee of the same color. For a man worth a small fortune he dresses like a dockhand: a plain blue linen shirt with the sleeves rolled up, simple brown trousers and a worn leather belt with a heavy coin purse hanging from it. Fine robes are for men who need to look rich. Goldie only needs to be rich, and he never pays a tailor for what a cheap shirt will do. His eyes are pale and attentive. When he smiles, it reaches them only when there's money involved.

Biography

Goldie was born with nothing in the old quarter of Stormwind, while the city was still rising from the rubble the Horde had left behind. His mother washed laundry for the masons, and his father wasn't around to be counted. As a boy he ran errands for anyone who would pay, and he learned early that coin moves the same way water does: always toward whoever is standing lower and asking for it the right way.

By fifteen he was buying cracked tools from the stonemasons cheap and selling them back mended. By twenty he was lending small sums to laborers at hard interest. When the House of Nobles refused to pay the Stonemasons' Guild and the masons left the city angry, Goldie bought up their unfinished contracts and sold them to the next crews. He has never felt guilty about it. Someone was going to profit, and it might as well be him.

Somewhere in those lean years the shadow found him. It didn't come from the Cathedral, and it never felt like a calling. It felt like a second pair of eyes. He learned he could feel a man's fear across a counting table, taste a lie before it was finished and sense the exact moment a desperate borrower would agree to anything. The Light never spoke to Goldie Sachs. The shadow speaks to him constantly, and what it says is always useful.

Today he invests in companies: shipping concerns on the canals, mining ventures in Dun Morogh and the Redridge hills, timber out of Elwynn and Duskwood, and a few trade houses he prefers not to name in public. He takes a share of the profits, and when a venture fails, he takes everything else. His townhouse sits a short walk from the Bank of Stormwind, close enough that he can watch who goes in looking worried.

He wants one thing, and he has never been shy about it. Goldie Sachs intends to become the richest man in all of Azeroth.

Personality

Goldie is charming, sharp and utterly ruthless. He doesn't raise his voice and he doesn't make threats. He doesn't need to. Debtors who miss a payment find their contracts called, their warehouses emptied and their names quietly passed around the Trade District. He considers mercy a bad investment and sentiment a cost nobody budgets for.

He is frugal to the point of comedy. He haggles over everything, tips no one and believes that buying a drink is the single worst trade a man can make. He has two weaknesses, booze and women, and he has solved the first one neatly: he drinks heavily, but only when someone else is paying. The second costs him more than he likes to admit, and he admits it to no one.

He has exactly one friend.

In character

  • He touches his hair when he's thinking, and some say when he's lying. He has never confirmed which.
  • He drinks at the Pig and Whistle often and has never once paid for a round.
  • Rumor in the Trade District says half the merchants on the canal owe him money, and the other half don't know it yet.
  • He tips bankers better than barmaids, because bankers remember.
  • His one good coat was tailored by Anouran Orestes, the only man in Stormwind he doesn't charge interest. They met when Goldie came to haggle over that coat and Anouran refused to drop the price. Goldie respected that so much he has been back every week since. Each of them has felt the shadow in the other. Neither has ever said a word about it.
  • He flips a gold coin across his knuckles while he talks business. Nobody has ever seen him drop it.
