Appearance

Jungen is a lean, loose-limbed Skyborne of twenty-five, with olive skin tanned darker by months on the road. The first thing anyone notices about him is his hair: a shock of bright orange, usually wind-tousled and full of whatever the last forest left in it. Past that, there is nothing about him that stands out. He has an ordinary face, an easy smile and eyes that never stay on one thing for long.

He dresses for walking, not for show: worn leathers, a patched cloak, soft boots and a satchel that is always too full. People who see him shift say his cat form keeps something of that hair, a coat with a faint orange sheen that catches the light like embers. His bear is broad, shaggy and much less graceful, and Jungen will be the first to say so.

Biography

In the high, windswept country of the Skyborne, the old ways were never written down. There are hunters and herders up there who still learn to listen to the mountain: to read the wind, to walk with the great bears of the peaks and to run with the lynxes along the cliffs. Jungen grew up among them. He was the child who wandered off, who came home late with scraped knees and a story about a fox den, and the elders took one look at him and knew where his restlessness should go.

They taught him what they knew: how to let his body change, how to wear the shape of a bear or a cat and come back to himself afterwards, and how to close a wound with a hand and a quiet word. They were honest with him that what they knew was small. Somewhere beyond the mountains, they said, there were others who had studied the wild for thousands of years.

That was all Jungen needed to hear. When he came of age, he left home with his satchel and no plan beyond "down, and then onward." Travellers' tales and a few patient night elves pointed him towards the Cenarion Circle and its sacred grove of Moonglade. He reached Nighthaven eventually, wide-eyed, half-starved and asking far too many questions, and the druids there took in the strange, orange-haired outsider as a student.

Moonglade gave him what his mountain teachers could not: names for the things he had always felt and a sense of how much more there was to learn. It also gave him a home, the first place he had found that felt as peaceful as the high country. He still considers it home today, and the Circle's teleport could take him there in a heartbeat. He almost never uses it. There is always another forest, another coastline, another ruin or river he hasn't seen yet.

So he walks. He has crossed the Wetlands, slept in the hills of Arathi and followed deer trails through Ashenvale. He doesn't travel to fight or to fulfil a duty, though he does both when the road demands it. He travels because the world is enormous and he wants to see as much of it as he can. Somewhere in Arathi, he once shared a campfire, and a long, mostly one-sided conversation, with a quiet Skyborne sellsword named Maynhard Lachrymore.

Personality

Jungen is curious about everything. He will stop mid-sentence to look at a beetle, ask a blacksmith how a hinge works or follow an interesting smell half a mile off the road. He asks questions the way other people breathe, and he actually listens to the answers.

He is also deeply easygoing. Rain, wrong turns, missed meals and strange looks at his hair all roll off him. He believes most problems look smaller after a good night's sleep under the stars, and he is usually right. That calm runs deep, but it isn't softness. Threaten something he cares about and the bear comes out, sometimes quite literally.

The Circle taught him about balance, and he takes it seriously in his own relaxed way: take only what you need, leave a place as you found it, and don't let the wild or the civilised world win entirely. He knows he is still a student, and he is happy to be one. To Jungen, not knowing something is just the start of a good afternoon.

In character

  • He doesn't drink. He sticks to juice, and he will happily tell you which inn presses the best apples in the Eastern Kingdoms.
  • He is easy to spot in a crowd because of the bright orange hair, and easy to lose because he has already wandered off to look at something.
  • He asks travellers where they're from and what it's like there, and he remembers the answers.
  • He sometimes slips into cat form just to cross a town faster, and doesn't always remember to change back before walking into a shop.
  • If you're hurt, he'll patch you up without being asked, though he's quick to say healing isn't really his strength.
  • He calls Moonglade home but can't remember the last time he was actually there.
