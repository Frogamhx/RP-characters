Appearance

Grudas is a tidy, well-groomed man with neatly kept dark hair and a warm smile. He always wears more layers than the weather calls for: a heavy coat, a scarf, sometimes gloves even indoors. His fingers are stained with ink and something chalky that never quite washes off. He looks like a friendly, slightly absent-minded scholar, the kind who would gladly talk for an hour about the properties of mageroyal. Under the smell of herbs and parchment there is a faint hint of something burnt.

Biography

The Lachrymores have always been a respectable family on paper: scholars, apothecaries and keepers of old books. For generations, they also quietly kept a second library of curse-craft, alchemical poisons and the theory of things that should not be summoned. Grudas grew up knowing that both libraries existed, and that only one of them was ever discussed at dinner.

As a grown man, he took his studies to Ironforge, where he’s known as a pleasant human alchemist who keeps odd hours. Far below the city, in tunnels the dwarves forgot long ago, he set up a workshop no one else knows about. There, out of pure curiosity, he developed a new kind of chalk, ground with reagents and bound with alchemy, to see whether a drawn line could hold a charge.

To test it, he drew a simple door on the stone wall.

It held more than a charge. The chalk lines began to glow, the wall stopped being a wall, and something small, red and very pleased with itself climbed through. Before Grudas could say a word, the imp grabbed the stick of chalk out of his hand and has refused to give it back ever since.

That became the deal between them: the imp keeps the chalk, and Grudas keeps the door. Every demon that has come through since has come through that same door, drawn with that same chalk, with the imp’s grudging help.

Something else came through that night, too: a cold he has never been able to shake. Whatever lies on the other side of the door is warm, and when it opened, some of his own warmth seems to have gone the other way. He hasn’t felt truly warm since. He takes the seat closest to every fire and tells people he simply “runs cold,” which is true enough.

His demons are not his slaves. They are his subordinates, a staff he expects to perform. Only he and his demons know what goes on below Ironforge.

His family is still alive, but he rarely writes to them. They have their library, and he has his door.

Personality

On the surface, Grudas is warm, chatty and quick to offer help, a drink or advice. The irony isn’t lost on him. Underneath, he is unforgiving. He doesn’t enjoy cruelty, but he has no patience for laziness, carelessness or incompetence. To him, a mistake in alchemy or summoning isn’t just embarrassing. It gets someone killed. He holds himself, his allies and his demons to the same standard.

He wants knowledge and the power that comes with it, but he has lines he won’t cross. He doesn’t harm innocents for his experiments, he keeps his word, and he never forgets that he is the one who opened the door.

In character

  • He’s often seen in the Stonefire Tavern, as close to the hearth as he can get, with a dwarven whiskey on the rocks. Why he takes it on ice when he’s always cold is a question he enjoys not answering.
  • He’s happy to talk about alchemy and will politely change the subject if anyone asks about curses.
  • He sometimes speaks to empty air in a low, firm voice, like a supervisor reviewing work.
  • His imp can occasionally be heard muttering about “the chalk,” which is apparently his chalk.
